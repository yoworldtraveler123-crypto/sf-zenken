Terms of Service

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. The service

The Add-on exports Salesforce reports into your own Google Sheets. Free features: CSV import and API fetch up to 2,000 rows. Licensed features (license key required): full export and daily auto refresh.

2. Data handling

  • Data flows directly between your spreadsheet and your Salesforce org. It never passes through the developer's or any third-party server, and the developer cannot access it.
  • The Add-on only reads from Salesforce. It never modifies or deletes data in your org.

3. Support

  • Support is provided by email (text) only. No phone or video support.
  • We aim to reply within 2 business days, but this is not guaranteed.
  • Reported bugs will be fixed on a best-effort basis; fixes ship automatically to all users as add-on updates.

4. Refunds

  • If the Add-on does not work despite following the setup guide, we will refund your license in full within 30 days of purchase.
  • We may ask for error details to verify the issue.
  • Purchases made through a marketplace platform follow that platform's refund process.

5. Requirements (not grounds for refund)

  • The target report must be in tabular format (summary/matrix formats are not supported)
  • The report must include a record ID column (or one specified in settings)
  • API export requires a connected app in Salesforce (admin required; we provide a request guide for your IT team)

6. Continuity

  • We make our best effort to fix defects.
