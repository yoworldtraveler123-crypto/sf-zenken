Salesforce reports in Google Sheets.
No 2,000-row limit.

ReportZen is a Google Sheets add-on that exports your Salesforce reports in full. It pages through your report automatically to break the 2,000-row cap of the native connector, and can refresh everything every morning.

What it does

  • FreeCSV import (no permissions needed — drag & drop)
  • FreeAPI report fetch up to 2,000 rows (great for trying it out)
  • LicensedFull report export — automatic paging, no row cap
  • LicensedDaily auto refresh & multi-report management

Your data never leaves your environment

  • Data flows directly between your spreadsheet and your Salesforce org — never through the developer's or any third-party server
  • Credentials are stored only in your own Google account's private property store
  • ReportZen only reads from Salesforce — it never modifies your org
