ReportZen vs. Google's Salesforce Connector (formerly Data Connector for Salesforce)

Both are add-ons that load Salesforce reports into Google Sheets. The main difference is reports over 2,000 rows. Google does not publish a row limit for its Salesforce Connector[1], but users keep reporting that report imports over 2,000 rows stop or fail[3][4]. ReportZen fetches the report in 2,000-row pages and writes every row. Google's connector, on the other hand, can write data back to Salesforce, import with SOQL, and refresh every 4 or 8 hours, which ReportZen cannot.

Which one to use

  • Reports under 2,000 rows, or you need write-back or SOQL: Google's Salesforce Connector is enough.
  • Reports over 2,000 rows, kept in report form, refreshed daily: ReportZen fits.
  • Your admin cannot create a connected app: Use Google's connector, or ReportZen's CSV import (drag and drop an exported CSV).
